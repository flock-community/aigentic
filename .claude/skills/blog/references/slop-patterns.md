# More patterns to cut

A checklist for the final check of a draft. It adds to the table in SKILL.md. It is a selection adapted from the
[AI Slop Detector](https://github.com/FeedHive/ai-slop-detector) by FeedHive (MIT licence); the full list of 120
patterns is at that link.

Use none of these in text you write or revise, and report them in a review. The word lists are about decoration. A
word used in its literal sense stays: "key" in "list key", "explore" for a search algorithm, "robust" in a quotation.

## Words

- delve, dive into, unpack, explore (for "discuss")
- landscape, realm, ecosystem, tapestry (used abstractly)
- pivotal, crucial, vital, critical, paramount, key (as decoration)
- testament to, hallmark of
- foster, cultivate, nurture (for abstract things)
- intricate, multifaceted, nuanced, complex (as filler)
- meticulous, carefully crafted
- vibrant, rich, dynamic, thriving
- align with, resonate with
- showcase, highlight, underscore (as commentary)
- utilise, harness, unlock, elevate, enhance, supercharge
- cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, best-in-class, world-class
- journey, embark, navigate (for an ordinary process)
- game changer, revolutionary, groundbreaking, transformative
- truly, incredibly, deeply, remarkably, fundamentally
- quietly, silently (to add drama to a plain event)
- Additionally, Furthermore, Moreover, Notably, Importantly (opening a sentence)

## Phrases

- it's worth noting, it's important to note
- at its core, at the end of the day, ultimately
- when it comes to, in terms of
- in today's fast-paced world, in an era of
- whether you're X or Y; "from X to Y" as a sweep of audiences or sizes ("from startups to enterprises"). A measured
  change ("from 85% to 98%") stays.
- navigate the complexities of, take it to the next level
- imagine a world where, picture this
- let's dive in, without further ado
- plays a role in, contributes to, serves as, stands as (for "is")
- here's the thing, the truth is, the reality is
- in conclusion, to sum up, overall (opening the last paragraph)
- the possibilities are endless, only time will tell

## Sentences

- "not just X, but also Y" and "The question isn't X, it's Y"
- "Not an X. Not a Y. A Z."
- "You might be wondering…"
- "what nobody tells you", "the part most people miss"
- "this is where it gets interesting"
- "the easy part", "the part that…"
- a sentence that ends in an -ing clause explaining its own meaning ("…, highlighting the need for testing")
- three adjectives or phrases in a row by reflex
- "one" before a noun when the number means nothing ("one surprise", "one change")
- boasts, features, offers (for "has")
- may potentially, could possibly, can sometimes
- "While X has benefits, it also has drawbacks"
- "X is just Y" with a vivid unrelated image
- "Or rather…", correcting yourself for effect
- an aside that tells the reader how to read the text ("this matters more than it sounds")
- "That's when it clicked", "And honestly…", "So yeah…"

## Shape

- a stack of one-line paragraphs
- strings of fragments ("Fast. Cheap. Done.")
- several sentences in a row that start with the same words
- every sentence the same length
- a long inventory of nouns in one sentence
- a heading over one or two sentences
- a table for two facts
- a closing "Despite these challenges, the outlook is positive"
- a list of generic lessons
- emoji as bullets or decoration

## Imagery

- data or products that "live" or "sit" somewhere
- systems that "tell the truth" or "hide" things
- two metaphors in one sentence
- fuel, engine, rocket, flywheel, secret sauce, silver bullet

## Substance

- inflated importance ("marks a pivotal moment", "represents a significant challenge")
- "studies show", "experts agree" without a source
- a sentence that could move unchanged to another company's blog
- a line that sounds deep and says nothing concrete
- a generic description in place of a sharp fact ("a major retailer" when the number of products is known)
- advice without steps ("optimise your prompts")
